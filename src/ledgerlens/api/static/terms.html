What thisdemonstration is.

LedgerLens is a demonstration. It runs on synthetic data generated from a fixed seed, and the figures it shows describe an invented insurer, an invented hiring funnel and invented vendors.

Nothing here is financial, legal or employment advice. The compliance documents the platform can draft are templates that illustrate a format, not counsel on your obligations.

The demonstration is provided as it stands, without warranty, and may change or go offline without notice.
