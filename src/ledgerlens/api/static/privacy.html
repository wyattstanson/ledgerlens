What wecollect: nothing.

This demonstration asks you for nothing. There is no sign-in, no account and no form that collects personal data.

No analytics or advertising scripts run on these pages. The only third parties your browser contacts are Google Fonts, jsDelivr for the chart and icon libraries, and the host that serves the site, each of which sees your IP address as part of delivering a file.

Questions the platform answers are matched in your browser against a fixed list and are not stored or sent to a language model.

The audit log records which tools ran, never who ran them, and it resets whenever the demonstration restarts.
